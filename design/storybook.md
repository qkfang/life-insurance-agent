# Storybook

One family, one city, one claim. A short picture-book walk through agentic life insurance.

## The city

| Place | Who is there |
| --- | --- |
| Tan home | Mia and Ben |
| Advisor office | Raj Iyer, advisor |
| Kestrel Life HQ | Agent team (drones) and human leads, e.g. Lena Ward, claims |

## The story

| # | Scene | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Home | Mia and Ben live a busy, ordinary life in the city. |
| 2 | The trigger | A life moment arrives: "We're having a baby." Who would cover the family if something happened? |
| 3 | The call | Mia calls Raj at the advisor office. |
| 4 | The compare | Raj compares offerings side by side and explains them in plain words. |
| 5 | Sign up | Mia uploads her documents and signs. The policy is issued. Life goes on. |
| 6 | The event | Months later, Ben gets bad news from the doctor. They need to claim. |
| 7 | The claim | Mia sends one message to Kestrel Life: "We need to make a claim." |
| 8 | The concierge | The Claims Concierge agent answers, opens the claim and says what comes next. |
| 9 | More documents | The concierge asks for a doctor's report. Mia uploads it from her phone. |
| 10 | The agents work | Agents check identity, gather evidence and build the assessment pack, step by step. |
| 11 | The human call | The core decision goes to Lena, a human. She reviews the pack and approves. |
| 12 | Sorted | Payment arrives at home. Mia sees "Claim paid" with every step explained. |
| 13 | Happy life | The family gets back to living, cover still in place. |

## The message

Agents do the legwork. Humans make the calls that matter. The family just lives.
